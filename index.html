2. The core principle: no data collection

Your privacy is not just a policy; it is the core principle of these apps. This document describes the commitment to protecting your personal information by not collecting it in the first place.

None of the apps listed above collects, stores, shares or transmits any personal data or sensitive user information to the developer.

The developer operates no servers, no user accounts, no databases and no backend of any kind. There is therefore no place where personal data about you could be stored. Your use of these apps is completely anonymous to the developer. To be perfectly clear, here is a non-exhaustive list of data that is NOT collected:

  • Your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact information.
  • Your device's unique identifiers (Device ID, Advertising ID, IMEI, etc.).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, calendars, messages or call logs.
  • Your photos, videos, audio or any files stored on your device.
  • Usage analytics, behavioural profiles, crash reports or telemetry of any kind.
  • Payment or financial information – the apps contain no purchases and no subscriptions.

This matches the "Data safety" declaration of every listed app on Google Play: no data collected, no data shared with third parties.

3. Data that stays on your device

Some apps need to remember things in order to work at all – for example your reminders in reReminder or your theme preference in Gsearch14. This information is stored exclusively in the app's private storage area on your own device, using standard Android mechanisms. It is:

  • never transmitted to the developer;
  • never transmitted to any third party;
  • not accessible to other apps on your device;
  • deleted completely when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Under data protection law this locally stored content is not "processed" by the developer, because the developer never has access to it.

4. Internet connections and third parties

Some apps must contact an external service to fulfil their core function – for example to retrieve live wait times or to display search results. In that case no data is sent to the developer, but a direct connection between your device and that external service takes place. As with any request on the internet, the operator of that service necessarily receives technical connection data such as your IP address, the time of the request and your user agent, and, where applicable, the content of your request (for example a search term you typed). This data is processed by that provider under its own privacy policy, over which the developer has no control.

The concrete services used by each app are listed in section 6 and on the app-specific pages. Beyond those, the apps contain:

  • no advertising networks and no ads;
  • no analytics or tracking SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Crashlytics, no attribution SDKs);
  • no social media SDKs;
  • no in-app purchases and no payment processing.

The distribution platform itself (Google Play) collects data about downloads and updates independently of these apps. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

An app only requests the Android permissions it needs for its own functionality. A permission is never used to gather information for the developer. Any data accessed through a permission remains exclusively on your device and is processed locally and only for the immediate purpose of the feature you are using. The permissions actually used are listed per app in section 6.

6. App-specific information

reReminder – com.olaf.rereminder

Purpose: a reminder app that repeatedly notifies you about healthy habits (drinking water, taking breaks, medication, movement).

Data stored on your device: the reminders you create (title, interval, schedule) and your app settings.

Data sent anywhere: none. The app works offline and does not need an internet connection for its core function.

Typical permissions: notifications (to display reminders), scheduling of exact alarms (so that a reminder fires on time), and restart after device reboot (so that your reminders survive a restart).

Full policy for this app

Gsearch14 – Search without AI – com.olafsapp.gsearch14

Purpose: an independent third-party utility that modifies your search query so that web search results are shown as a plain list of links instead of AI-generated summaries, and opens them in an in-app browser.

Data stored on your device: your app settings (for example theme) and, if the app offers this, your local search history. This never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app data or uninstall the app.

Data sent to third parties: the search term you enter is sent to the search engine (Google Search) in order to return results, exactly as it would be in any browser. When you open a result, your device contacts the website you selected. Those providers receive your IP address and the usual technical request data and process it under their own privacy policies – see the Google Privacy Policy for Google Search. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Note: Gsearch14 is an independent utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Google LLC.

Typical permissions: internet access.

Full policy for this app

Phantaland Queue Times – com.quantum_prof.phantalandwaittimes

Purpose: an unofficial app that shows current wait times for the attractions of the Phantasialand theme park.

Data stored on your device: your app settings (for example filters and sort order) and, where applicable, a temporary cache of the wait-time data.

Data sent to third parties: to display wait times, the app requests them from a publicly accessible third-party wait-time API. The request contains no personal data and no identifier of you; it is a plain request for public park data. The API operator receives the technical connection data described in section 4 (in particular your IP address) and processes it under its own privacy policy. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Note: this is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Phantasialand.

Typical permissions: internet access.

Full policy for this app

7. Children

These apps are not directed at children under the age of 13 in particular, but they are safe for all age groups in terms of data protection: since no personal data whatsoever is collected, no data of children can be collected either. There is no user-generated content, no chat, no social feature and no advertising.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the developer never receives or stores any personal data about you, there is no data set that could be disclosed, corrected or deleted. You can exercise the practical equivalent of erasure at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app: everything the app has stored is then gone.

If you are a resident of California, the same applies to your rights under the CCPA/CPRA. No personal information is collected, sold or shared.

9. Open source transparency (FOSS)
