1. What this app does

reReminder is a simple, open source reminder app. It notifies you repeatedly about healthy habits – drinking water, taking breaks from screen work, taking medication on schedule or getting up and moving.

The app is Free and Open Source Software (FOSS), developed as a private, non-commercial hobby project. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

2. No data collection

This app collects, stores, shares or transmits NO personal data or sensitive user information to the developer.

The developer operates no server, no user account system and no database, so there is no place where personal data about you could be stored. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact information.
  • Your device's unique identifiers (Device ID, Advertising ID, IMEI, etc.).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, calendars, messages or call logs.
  • Your photos, videos, audio or other files on your device.
  • Usage analytics, behavioural profiles, crash reports or telemetry of any kind.

This matches the „Data safety“ declaration of this app on Google Play: no data collected, no data shared with third parties.

3. Data that stays on your device

The reminders you create (title, interval, schedule) and your app settings are stored in the app's private storage area on your device only.

This information is never transmitted to the developer or to a third party, is not accessible to other apps, and is deleted completely when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Internet connections and third parties

reReminder works 100 % offline. The app does not even request the Android internet permission, so it is technically unable to send any data to the developer or to any third-party server. It contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind.

The help screen contains links to dontkillmyapp.com with tips for devices that stop background apps. These links open in your own browser only when you tap them.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications – needed to display the reminders on your device.
  • Exact alarms / scheduling – needed so that a reminder actually fires at the time you set.
  • Start after device reboot – needed so that your reminders keep working after you restart your phone.
  • Full-screen notifications – only for reminders you set up as an alarm, so they can be shown over the lock screen.
  • Vibration and wake lock – to vibrate and to keep the device awake for the moment a reminder is played.
  • Foreground service – so that an alarm-style reminder keeps playing reliably until you dismiss it.
  • Request to ignore battery optimisations – Android asks you for confirmation; it only prevents the system from silently killing your reminders.

None of these permissions is used to read information about you or to send anything anywhere. They only allow the app to alert you at the right moment.

6. Security

reReminder follows a simple security principle: data that never leaves your device cannot be stolen, sold or leaked.

  • No internet permission. The app cannot open any network connection. This is enforced by Android itself, not just promised.
  • No developer server. There is no backend that could be hacked, leak data or be forced to hand over data – because none exists.
  • Android app sandbox. Everything the app stores lives in its private storage area, which other apps cannot access.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only the permissions listed above and no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data. The app is built only from open source libraries (AndroidX/Jetpack and Kotlin) – no Google Play Services, no Firebase, no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • Public, reviewable code. Every change is visible in the public Git history and checked by automated builds (GitHub Actions).

Found a security issue? Please report it confidentially by e-mail to makerlab.fffm@gmail.com. Reports are taken seriously and fixed as quickly as possible.

7. Children

The app is safe for all age groups from a data protection point of view: since no personal data is collected at all, no data of children can be collected either. There is no user-generated content, no chat and no advertising.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the developer never receives or stores personal data about you, there is no data set that could be disclosed, corrected or deleted. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored locally.
