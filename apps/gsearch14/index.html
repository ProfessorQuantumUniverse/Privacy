1. What this app does

Gsearch14 is an independent third-party utility that modifies your web search query so that results are shown as a clean, link-focused list instead of AI-generated summaries. Results open in an in-app browser.

The app is Free and Open Source Software (FOSS), developed as a private, non-commercial hobby project. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

2. No data collection

This app collects, stores, shares or transmits NO personal data or sensitive user information to the developer.

The developer operates no server, no user account system and no database, so there is no place where personal data about you could be stored. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact information.
  • Your device's unique identifiers (Device ID, Advertising ID, IMEI, etc.).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, calendars, messages or call logs.
  • Your photos, videos, audio or other files on your device.
  • Usage analytics, behavioural profiles, crash reports or telemetry of any kind.

This matches the „Data safety“ declaration of this app on Google Play: no data collected, no data shared with third parties.

3. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored in the app's private storage area on your device only:

  • your app settings (for example the selected search engine and the theme);
  • your search history and your bookmarks – you can clear the history at any time, and in incognito mode no history is recorded;
  • like every browser, the in-app browser may keep cookies and a cache of the websites you visit on your device; cookies can be blocked in the settings.

Apart from cookies, which the in-app browser returns only to the website that set them (as every browser does), this information is never transmitted to the developer or to a third party, is not accessible to other apps, and is deleted completely when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Internet connections and third parties

Gsearch14 needs the internet to do its job. Three kinds of connection happen, and in all cases the connection goes directly from your device to the service concerned – never through a server of the developer:

  • Your search term is sent to the search engine you selected – Google Search, Marginalia Search or Wikipedia – so that it can return results, exactly as it would be if you typed the same query into a browser.
  • Search suggestions: while you type, the text typed so far is sent to the suggestion service of the selected engine (Google, Wikipedia, or DuckDuckGo for Marginalia, which has none of its own). You can switch suggestions off in the settings; then nothing is sent until you start the search.
  • When you open a result, your device contacts that website.

These providers necessarily receive technical connection data (in particular your IP address, the time of the request and your user agent) and the content of your request, and they process it under their own privacy policies – see the Google Privacy Policy, the Wikimedia Privacy Policy, the Marginalia Search website and the DuckDuckGo Privacy Policy. The developer receives none of this and cannot see what you search for.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind.

5. Permissions

  • Internet access – needed to perform searches and to display web pages in the in-app browser.
  • Network state – to check whether a network connection is available before a search is started.

6. Security

Gsearch14 is designed so that the developer is never in the path of your searches:

  • Direct connections only. Your device talks directly to the search engine and websites you choose. No proxy, no developer server, nothing in between.
  • Encrypted search requests. All requests the app itself makes to search engines and suggestion services use HTTPS.
  • Privacy controls in your hands. Incognito mode (no history), cookie blocking for the in-app browser, switchable search suggestions and one-tap history deletion.
  • No developer server. There is no backend that could be hacked, leak data or be forced to hand over data – because none exists.
  • Android app sandbox. Everything the app stores lives in its private storage area, which other apps cannot access.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only internet and network-state access – no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data. The app is built only from open source libraries (AndroidX/Jetpack and Kotlin) – no Google Play Services, no Firebase, no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • Public, reviewable code. Every change is visible in the public Git history and checked by automated builds (GitHub Actions).

Found a security issue? Please report it confidentially by e-mail to makerlab.fffm@gmail.com. Reports are taken seriously and fixed as quickly as possible.

7. Children

The app is safe for all age groups from a data protection point of view: since no personal data is collected at all, no data of children can be collected either. There is no user-generated content, no chat and no advertising.

8. Your rights

Because the developer never receives or stores personal data about you, there is no data set that could be disclosed, corrected or deleted. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored locally.
