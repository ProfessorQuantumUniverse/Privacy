This document is the privacy policy referenced in the Google Play Store listing of this app.

1. What this app does

Gsearch14 is an independent third-party utility that modifies your web search query so that results are shown as a clean, link-focused list instead of AI-generated summaries. Results open in an in-app browser.

The app is Free and Open Source Software (FOSS), developed as a private, non-commercial hobby project. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

2. No data collection

This app collects, stores, shares or transmits NO personal data or sensitive user information to the developer.

The developer operates no server, no user account system and no database, so there is no place where personal data about you could be stored. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact information.
  • Your device's unique identifiers (Device ID, Advertising ID, IMEI, etc.).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, calendars, messages or call logs.
  • Your photos, videos, audio or other files on your device.
  • Usage analytics, behavioural profiles, crash reports or telemetry of any kind.

This matches the „Data safety“ declaration of this app on Google Play: no data collected, no data shared with third parties.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your app settings (for example the light/dark theme) and, where the app offers it, your local search history are stored in the app's private storage area on your device only. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app removes them completely.

This information is never transmitted to the developer or to a third party, is not accessible to other apps, and is deleted completely when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Internet connections and third parties

Gsearch14 needs the internet to do its job. Two kinds of connection happen, and in both cases the connection goes directly from your device to the service concerned – never through a server of the developer:

  • Your search term is sent to the search engine (Google Search) so that it can return results, exactly as it would be if you typed the same query into a browser.
  • When you open a result, your device contacts that website.

These providers necessarily receive technical connection data (in particular your IP address, the time of the request and your user agent) and the content of your request, and they process it under their own privacy policies – for Google Search see the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives none of this and cannot see what you search for.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no tracking of any kind.

5. Permissions

  • Internet access – needed to perform searches and to display web pages in the in-app browser.

6. Children

The app is safe for all age groups from a data protection point of view: since no personal data is collected at all, no data of children can be collected either. There is no user-generated content, no chat and no advertising.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the developer never receives or stores personal data about you, there is no data set that could be disclosed, corrected or deleted. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored locally.

8. Open source transparency

You do not have to take these statements on trust: the source code is public and can be inspected at github.com/ProfessorQuantumUniverse.

Disclaimer
