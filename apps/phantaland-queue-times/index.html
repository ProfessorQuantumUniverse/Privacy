1. What this app does

Phantaland Queue Times is an unofficial, open source app that shows the current wait times for the attractions of the Phantasialand theme park.

The app is Free and Open Source Software (FOSS), developed as a private, non-commercial hobby project. It contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no user accounts.

2. No data collection

This app collects, stores, shares or transmits NO personal data or sensitive user information to the developer.

The developer operates no server, no user account system and no database, so there is no place where personal data about you could be stored. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, e-mail address, phone number or any other contact information.
  • Your device's unique identifiers (Device ID, Advertising ID, IMEI, etc.).
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your contacts, calendars, messages or call logs.
  • Your photos, videos, audio or other files on your device.
  • Usage analytics, behavioural profiles, crash reports or telemetry of any kind.

This matches the „Data safety“ declaration of this app on Google Play: no data collected, no data shared with third parties.

3. Data that stays on your device

Your app settings (for example filters and sort order), the wait-time alerts you set (attraction and target wait time) and, where applicable, a short-lived cache of the wait-time data are stored in the app's private storage area on your device only.

This information is never transmitted to the developer or to a third party, is not accessible to other apps, and is deleted completely when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Internet connections and third parties

To show live wait times, the app requests them from the publicly accessible wait-time API of wartezeiten.app (api.wartezeiten.app). The request is a plain request for public park data: it contains no personal data, no account and no identifier of you or your device. While a wait-time alert is active, Android's background scheduler (WorkManager) repeats this same request from time to time so the app can notify you locally when the wait drops.

As with any internet request, the operator of that API necessarily receives technical connection data – in particular your IP address, the time of the request and your user agent – and processes it under its own privacy policy. The developer has no access to this data and operates no server of their own.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no in-app purchases and no tracking of any kind.

5. Permissions

  • Internet access – needed to load the current wait times.
  • Notifications – optional; only used to tell you when an attraction reaches the wait time you set.

6. Security

Phantaland Queue Times only reads public information, so there is very little to protect – and that little is protected well:

  • Encrypted connections. The wait-time API is contacted exclusively over HTTPS.
  • Nothing personal in requests. The requests contain no account, no identifier and no personal data – only „give me the current wait times“.
  • Alerts are evaluated on your device. Your wait-time alerts are stored and checked locally; nobody else knows which rides you are waiting for.
  • No developer server. There is no backend that could be hacked, leak data or be forced to hand over data – because none exists.
  • Android app sandbox. Everything the app stores lives in its private storage area, which other apps cannot access.
  • Least privilege. The app requests only internet access and (optionally) notifications – no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data. The app is built only from open source libraries (AndroidX/Jetpack and Kotlin, Retrofit, OkHttp, Hilt) – no Google Play Services, no Firebase, no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • Public, reviewable code. Every change is visible in the public Git history and checked by automated builds (GitHub Actions).

Found a security issue? Please report it confidentially by e-mail to makerlab.fffm@gmail.com. Reports are taken seriously and fixed as quickly as possible.

7. Children

The app is safe for all age groups from a data protection point of view: since no personal data is collected at all, no data of children can be collected either. There is no user-generated content, no chat and no advertising.

8. Your rights

Because the developer never receives or stores personal data about you, there is no data set that could be disclosed, corrected or deleted. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes everything the app has stored locally.
